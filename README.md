# CHATDOOM

**Your live chat, read aloud, so you never have to look away from the game.**

CHATDOOM is a free Windows app for live streamers. It reads your YouTube, Twitch, Kick and Facebook chat out loud in
natural Hindi, Hinglish and English voices, keeps the chat clean, and quietly helps you run the stream while you play.
It is made for streamers who game or talk on camera and can't keep their eyes on chat all the time.

**What it does**
- **Reads chat aloud** from several platforms at once, with 300+ voices, translation and per-viewer voices.
- **Keeps chat safe:** skips spam, links and blocked words (even when they are misspelled), shields you from hate raids,
  and never reads phone numbers or UPI IDs aloud.
- **Watches chat for you:** collects questions, gives you a spoken catch-up after a tense round, and marks hype moments for clips.
- **AI co-host** that answers viewers in their own language, plus `!commands`, memes and Valorant stats.
- **OBS overlays** for alerts, chat, goals and a floating "ghost audience", and alerts for Super Chats, tips and subscribers.
- **Discord tools:** roles for viewers and a live announcement when you go live.

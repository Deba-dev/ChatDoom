PAYMENTS  -  your own UPI tip page and QR code. Viewers pay by UPI; CHATDOOM reads the name, amount and message aloud
and shows an alert in OBS. Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments (RBI-authorised payment aggregator) and Cashfree
pays you directly to your bank account or UPI ID, two working days later. ZunimiLabs never holds your money.

Each tip: you get 98%, ZunimiLabs keeps 2%. Viewers pay by UPI only. The smallest tip is up to you (at least Rs 10).

DO FIRST: the Discord page (server key). Tips use the same connection.

SET UP (once, about 5 minutes)
1. CHATDOOM > Payments > UPI tip page.
2. Fill in: page name (your link becomes cloud.zunimilabs.online/t/<page name>), the name viewers see, the smallest tip,
   where to get paid (UPI ID, or bank account + IFSC), the account holder's name exactly as the bank has it, PAN,
   email and mobile number. Click "Save my tip page".
   Your PAN and account number go to Cashfree only (it needs them to pay you); ZunimiLabs does not keep them.
3. Cashfree checks your account, usually within a day. The page says "Live" when it is ready.
4. Put the QR on stream: click "Copy QR link for OBS", then in OBS add a Browser source with that address, 400 x 400.
   Or share your page link in chat and your channel description.
5. Payments > Read aloud: tick "Listen for tips and read them aloud". "Send a test tip" tries the alert.

HOW IT BEHAVES
- A message with a blocked word is never read or shown; the name and amount still are.
- INR tips below the "minimum amount for the message" show/read the name and amount without the message.
- The same tip is never read twice. Alerts normally arrive within a few seconds of the viewer paying.
- "Pause tips" closes your page until you resume it.
- To change your page name or title later, change them and Save; leave the payout fields empty to keep your account.
  To change where you get paid, fill in the payout fields again (with your PAN) and Save.

Already connected a Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree or custom webhook in an older version? It keeps working; there is just
no setup screen for new ones any more.
